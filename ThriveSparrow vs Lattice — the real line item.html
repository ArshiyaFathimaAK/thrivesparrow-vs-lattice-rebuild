The same Tuesday, two platforms

A people ops manager wants to recognize a team that just shipped something hard, and surface whether morale is actually holding up underneath the praise.

With Lattice

  1. Posts public recognition in Lattice's praise feed.
  2. Opens a separate pulse-survey add-on (or a third-party tool) to check engagement.
  3. Exports two sets of results and reconciles them manually before the Monday leadership sync.
Roughly three tools, one spreadsheet.

With ThriveSparrow

  1. Sends Kudos with points attached, redeemable in the built-in rewards marketplace.
  2. Launches a two-minute pulse check from the same dashboard.
  3. Reads AI sentiment analysis on the open-text responses before the sync even starts.
One login, one afternoon.

Where Lattice still wins

We'd be overselling it to say ThriveSparrow is the better fit for every team. Lattice has a longer track record with large, multi-thousand-employee enterprises, a bigger marketplace of HRIS integrations, and more name recognition in board-level conversations.

If your HR stack is already deeply built around Lattice's ecosystem, or you need integrations we don't yet support, switching has a real cost. ThriveSparrow is the stronger case for mid-sized, growth-stage teams who want engagement, recognition, and performance under one roof without assembling it themselves.
